# Title ideas

> **Current title: Blacktop Outlaws** (the default `GAME_NAME`). See "About Blacktop Outlaws" at the bottom. Concept art is in `concept-art/`. Earlier candidates (Sk8, Local Skater, etc.) are kept below for reference.

The game name is one setting (`GAME_NAME`), so nothing is locked in. Ideas lean on what makes this game different: **real skateparks, scanned, and you represent yours.**

**Top picks**
1. **Park Rep** — says the whole hook in two words ("rep your park"). Short, easy to say, easy as a handle.
2. **Home Spot** — warm and local; "my home spot" is how skaters actually talk.
3. **Concrete Crew** — leans on the crew/community side; alliteration sticks.

**More**
- **Rep Your Park** — the tagline as a title.
- **Spot Check** — skate slang plus "checking in" at parks; works with the check-in feature.
- **Every Park** / **Skate Every Park** — suits the "we visit parks across the country" tour.
- **Hometown Shred** — friendly, kid-appropriate.
- **Locals** — simple; likely crowded, so would need a subtitle (e.g. *Locals: Rep Your Park*).
- **Lines** — skate term for a run of tricks; elegant but hard to search for.
- **Kickturn Kids** — leans into the kid audience (check it doesn't read as a toy brand).

**Avoid:** *Session* and *skate.* are existing skateboarding games; *Skate City*, *Skater XL*, *OlliOlli*, and *Tony Hawk's* are taken too. Also skip anything with "Thrasher"/"Vans"/other brand names.

## What I checked (and didn't)
I did a quick web search on *Local Spot*, *Concrete Crew*, *Park Rep / Rep Your Park*, *Home Spot*, *Hometown Sessions*, and *Spot Check* as skateboarding games and found nothing by those names (the nearest were *Session: Skate Sim*, EA's *skate.*, *Skate City*, and a tiny itch.io puzzle game, *Concrete Chad*). **That is not a trademark search.** Before you commit to any name: search the USPTO database, Steam/itch/app stores, domain availability, and social handles (@Jmystigan style), and ask a lawyer to clear it if you'll sell it.

## How to choose
Say it out loud in a sentence a kid would use ("Wanna play ___?"), check it's easy to type, and check you can get the domain + a matching handle. Then set `GAME_NAME` and (if the studio is renamed too) `STUDIO_NAME` — the site, emails, and launcher branding all follow.

## About "Sk8"
It's short, fits the culture, and looks great as a logo. Things to know before committing:
- **It's already used a lot.** A quick search found *Thrasher SK8* (PlayStation, Japan), *Tony Hawk's American Sk8land*, *Street Sk8er*, and a planned *SK8* from Rage Software, plus a board called "SK8" in Nintendo's WarioWare. Mostly old, but it means players and search engines won't see "Sk8" as yours.
- **Weak as a trademark.** "Sk8" is just a common spelling of "skate", so it's hard to protect and easy to be confused with EA's *skate.* A past USPTO registration for SK8 in video games was cancelled in 2015, but I couldn't confirm whether anything current exists — that needs a proper USPTO search.
- **Hard to find.** Searching "sk8" returns skate gear, anime, and old games. Pair it with something distinctive so people can actually find *your* game.
- **What I'd do:** keep **Sk8** as the brand but add a distinctive hook: **Sk8: Park Rep**, **Sk8 Local**, or **Sk8 Crew**. Then check the USPTO database, Steam/itch/app stores, the domain, and your social handles, and have a lawyer clear it.

To change it any time: set `GAME_NAME` (everything — site, emails, launcher — follows).

## About "Blacktop Outlaws"
- **Fit:** distinctive, western + street, and easy to build a look around (see `concept-art/blacktop-outlaws.png`). Much more ownable than "Sk8" or "Local Skater".
- **Quick search found no game with this exact name.** Neighbours to be aware of: *Outlaws* (1997 western shooter, remastered by Atari in late 2025), and an in-development indie driving game called *Blacktop*. Sharing a word with each isn't a conflict by itself, but they're in the same "game" space, so a proper trademark search matters.
- **Community angle:** the name says "outlaw" but your app is about *crews* and *representing your park*. A tagline can carry that: e.g. "Rep your park" / "Every park has a crew".
- **Kids angle:** "Outlaws" is a playful western term, not violent, but it's worth checking how it reads for parents and for app-store age ratings (IARC/ESRB) — keep the imagery skate-and-adventure, not weapons.
- **To do before spending money:** USPTO search (games, apparel), Steam/itch/app stores, domain (e.g. blacktopoutlaws.com), social handles, then a lawyer.
